Kabe is a nutrition, fitness and lifestyle app. This page says what the app stores, what leaves your phone, and your choices.

## Stored on your phone
Food diary, weight, water, workouts, steps, activity, progress photos, reminders and settings are stored on your device. Progress photos never leave it.

## What can leave your phone
- **AI features (meal photos, voice, menus, labels, weekly summary, tips, food and vitamin lookups).** When you use one, the photo, recording or text needed for that request, and for some features a summary of your recent logs, is sent to Kabe's server, which forwards it to Google's Gemini API and returns the answer. Google's free tier may use submitted content to improve its products, so do not photograph anything private. You can switch AI sharing off any time in Me → Privacy.
- **Packaged-food barcodes** are looked up on Open Food Facts when you scan one.
- **Friends and challenges.** If you use them, your chosen display name, this week's totals (steps, active minutes, days with food logged, points) and any meal plan you choose to share are stored on Kabe's server and shown to people who hold your friend code.
- **Account and backup (optional).** If you create an account, your diary data is backed up to Kabe's server, encrypted in transit and readable only by your account. You can delete the backup, or the whole account, in the app.
- **A guest session.** Without an account the app starts an anonymous guest session so the AI and friends features work. It holds no name, email or diary data.

## What is not collected
No advertising, no tracking, no selling of data. Step counts and GPS routes stay on your phone unless you save an activity and back up with an account.

## Your choices
Switch off AI sharing, delete cloud data, delete your account, or erase everything in Me. Deleting the account removes your server data, including friends and shared plans.

## Health information
Kabe's calorie and nutrition numbers are estimates and are not medical advice.
